The problem

Your website is open even when your office isn't.

A customer doesn't always visit your website during business hours. They might have a plumbing problem at night, be comparing local plumbers on a weekend, or simply have a question before they're ready to call. If they can't get an answer, there's a good chance they move on.

What the assistant can do

More than a chatbot. A helpful first conversation.

The goal isn't to replace your team. It's to handle the simple questions that often happen before a customer calls, books, or sends an enquiry.

01

Answer Common Questions

Customers can ask about services, opening hours, service areas, emergency plumbing, pricing, appointments and more.

02

Help After Hours

Someone visiting your website late at night can still get useful information instead of waiting until the next morning.

03

Guide Visitors

The assistant can help visitors figure out what service they may need and guide them toward the next step.

04

Capture Enquiries

When someone needs help, the assistant can collect their details so the business can follow up.

05

Respond Instantly

Customers don't have to wait for an email response just to find out whether you provide a particular service.

06

Keep Your Team Focused

The assistant handles repetitive questions while your team focuses on actual plumbing jobs and customers.
